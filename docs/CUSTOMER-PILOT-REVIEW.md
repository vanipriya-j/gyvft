# Copyable GYVFT pilot review brief

Review https://www.gyvft.com as a first-time gift buyer and as a GYVFT operator. Assess the implemented product, not just the static mock-up. Ask me for private access details if needed; do not invent credentials. The fictional demo accounts cover personal gifting, a business, several communities and a family. Do not publish passwords in your report.

The pilot deliberately accepts manual Operations, payment verification and Shopify handoffs. Judge whether the customer receives a clear, reliable service while the team works manually. Do not classify an agreed manual process or a deferred feature as a launch-blocking defect.

For business gifting, also review `/business-review.html` against `docs/BUSINESS-ENTITIES-AND-DELIVERY.md`. This is a separate proposal, not production billing functionality. Check the individual/circle/legal-entity distinction, explicit purchasing authority, GST registrations, registered versus billing/office addresses, immutable historical billing and delivery independent of bill-to. Office bulk delivery must not require employee home addresses. Split and individual deliveries need quantity allocation and truthful partial delivery. Do not assume the existing B2C Shopify adapter supports company billing or multiple destinations.

Our benchmark is a small application with the clarity, reliability and accessibility expected of a mature product. Every element needs a purpose. The design should feel warm and energetic: cream backgrounds, near-black text, red/olive actions, compact spacing, a clear primary action, cohesive page headers, stable layouts and deliberate mobile compositions. Identify redundancy, visual hierarchy problems, awkward empty states and dead-end journeys. Do not propose an unrelated visual redesign.

Walk these journeys on desktop and a narrow mobile viewport:

1. Sign in and recover access. Check truthful feedback, errors, navigation/loading states and whether controls respond without repeated clicks. Registration/reset delivery must be distinguished from a 200 response or a generic success message.
2. Home → My gifts → gift detail → options → select a curated option or customise a copy → request/approve quote → approve artwork → pay the advance → payment verification → production → balance → delivery → testimonial/photos. Check each tab, return path, edit boundary and next action. Options are visual gift directions; the quote is a separate priced commitment. Published options stay immutable; customisation creates a customer copy. Show/hide is optional, not a mandatory shortlist workflow.
3. Check money clarity: “50% now; remaining 50% before dispatch.” Taxes and included product printing must be clear. Packaging and delivery are additional; delivery is at actuals. Shared design direction/minor changes are complimentary for orders above ₹4,000; smaller orders require a conversation. Additional name/message personalisation is separately priced. Changes stop once production starts. Payment screenshots require manual verification, allowing up to 24 hours; submitting proof is not payment confirmation.
4. Occasions → occasion detail/history → plan a gift. Occasion date and requested delivery date are separate. Circles begin with the individual; a person can belong to several family, community or institutional circles. Verify real membership and correctly linked occasions, invitations, admin boundaries, opt-in profile sharing and private recipient notes. Organisation validation is simple and separate from membership. An email domain alone must not grant organisation authority.
5. Profile, people, tastes and account access. No compulsory circle/profile setup before creating a gift. Check per-photo public permission, including withdrawing permission for one photo independently. Private gifts, money and notes must remain private.
6. Operations dashboard → queue → same request → publish options/quotes/artwork → reply → verify/reject proof → update production/dispatch/delivery. Identify who should act next and what the customer sees after every action. Check customer/team access boundaries, search, filters, stable page transitions and loading states. Shopify is the order-management source of truth when linked, with one custom gift-batch line attached to the customer.
7. Email: confirm relevant customer/team events produce the right notification, safe private links, truthful accepted-versus-delivered states, useful failure visibility and no duplicate sends. Manual Ops updates should notify customers. WhatsApp is deferred. Demo requests intentionally do not send real messages or create real payments/Shopify orders; do not mistake that safeguard for a notification failure.

Use only fictional test data for writes. Do not run migrations, delete/reset data, send real customer messages, charge money or create real Shopify orders without explicit approval. If a journey cannot be verified safely, mark it unverified and explain what evidence is needed. Do not claim browser verification from code inspection alone.

For each finding, provide severity, role, exact URL/state, reproducible steps, expected/actual behavior, screenshot or other evidence, user impact and a concrete fix. Separate defects from agreed product decisions and integration checks that remain unverified. Check 44px practical touch controls, keyboard access, visible focus, named dialogs, contrast, status announcements, scroll/overflow and connection failures.

Finish with the five highest-impact fixes and a clear judgment: ready for a guided customer preview, ready for a manually operated paid pilot, or blocked—and name the actual customer-visible blockers. Avoid another broad redesign unless the observed tasks justify it.
