# BITS 2001 Reunion Yearbook

On 24 November 2026 the owner supplied four references: the packaged finished yearbook with its campus sticker sheet; the unwrapped front cover; a full-cover visual; and the open printed campus illustration. The instruction was to restage the raw images in GYVFT’s visual language and publish the result. This updates the existing `/stories/bits-2001-reunion` page; it does not create a duplicate story or change its category order.

## Presentation

Two matching images use a warm ivory tabletop, soft natural light and the book’s original blue-sky/pink-bougainvillea palette. Office clutter, hands and plastic packaging are removed. They are labelled **styled product mockups**, rather than represented as unedited photographs. Image generation restages the source and may reinterpret fine details; the raw references remain the authority for production artwork.

- `hero.webp`: the closed yearbook and separate campus sticker sheet. The physical front-cover wording, “Batch of 2001 / 25 YEARS REUNION / YEARBOOK”, is the reference, rather than the different wording on the full-cover study.
- `illustrated-spread.webp`: the open campus illustration, preserving the brown line-art direction and green/yellow accents from the physical book.
- Original interim `hero.png` is retained at its existing URL. No original image or source archive is deleted.

The web assets are converted from generated PNGs to quality-90 WebP at their original 1536 × 1024 dimensions. No runtime filesystem traversal, migration or customer-data operation is introduced.

Combined image payload: 758,608 bytes. Build, TypeScript and changed-file lint checks pass. Chromium checks at 320, 390 and 1440 pixels confirm that both images load without cropping or horizontal overflow, the heading is visible with reduced motion, and the page has no JavaScript errors. Listing checks confirm Orient is first, all 17 stories appear exactly once and the earlier category order is preserved. A record comparison against production’s prior commit confirms the other 15 existing stories and all redirects remain unchanged.

## Narrative

The case study describes the 25-year reunion, the yearbook, campus illustrations and accompanying stickers visible in the supplied references. No page count, print quantity, price, recipient testimonial or invented account of the reunion is added. Personal alumni profile pages are not exposed.

## Release scope

The owner’s “put it out there” instruction authorises publication. The Stories collection keeps Orient first as requested, shows it only once, and retains the other existing stories in their category order. The existing homepage’s selected stories are unchanged.
